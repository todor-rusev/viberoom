Your voice

Speak instead of typing: the microphone in the message field turns what you say into words, or sends the recording itself as a voice message. The vibemates can also read replies aloud. A speech service of your choice does the hearing and the reading; viberoom sends it only the recordings and the replies you choose.

You set it up in Settings → Voice, or ask a vibemate to walk you through it. Nothing here is a recommendation: the right service is the one that fits what matters to you.

What to weigh

  • How exact it is in your language, with your accent and with the names you use. A larger model is usually more exact and slower.
  • What it costs. Most services charge by the minute of audio; some have a free allowance; a server on your own computer costs nothing per minute but needs a capable computer.
  • Where your voice goes. A service on the internet receives each recording; a server on this computer keeps it here.
  • How fast the words come back, and whether it can also read replies aloud in your language.

The choices in Settings → Voice

  • OpenAI: its own speech models, for hearing and for reading aloud. A key from platform.openai.com.
  • Groq: Whisper models on Groq's servers. A key from console.groq.com.
  • Your own server: any server or service with OpenAI's audio API. A Whisper server on this computer keeps your voice here; you give its address and the name of its model.

Each service names its own models, and their prices change; its own pages say what it offers today. The model field shows an example; any model the service lists for speech works.

Other services with OpenAI's audio API also go under Your own server. For example OpenRouter: the endpoint https://openrouter.ai/api/v1, a model name with its maker in front, such as openai/whisper-1, and an OpenRouter key. It does not use the names viberoom gives to help with spelling. A transcription request may time out after 60 seconds of processing; that is processing time, not a one-minute limit on the recording.

When nothing is heard

viberoom measures each recording. When the microphone gave only silence, nothing is sent and you are told so. The usual reasons:

  • The browser may not use the microphone. The browser asks the first time; a refusal stays in its site settings for this page.
  • The system may not let the browser use it. On Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone, with access for desktop apps on. On macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, with your browser allowed. On Linux: the sound settings' input; a browser installed as a Flatpak or a Snap also needs the microphone allowed for it.
  • Another microphone is chosen, or it is muted: the system's sound settings show which input is used and whether it moves when you speak.

What this does not do

  • It does not listen on its own: the microphone records only between your presses, and a recording you cancel goes nowhere.
  • It does not show your key to anyone: it is kept encrypted on this computer and never shown again.
