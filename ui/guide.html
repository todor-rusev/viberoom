Your phone, your rooms

Your rooms live on this computer. A Telegram bot carries what you write on the phone to a room here, and the vibemates' replies back to the phone. The bot is yours; this computer answers for it.

Before you start: Telegram on your phone, signed in. Nothing else — you make the bot in it, in a minute.

About ten minutes. Once it is on, the messages of any room you open on the phone travel through Telegram.

Set up Telegram… opens the wizard where you left it

1 · Make the bot

In Telegram, open BotFather and send /newbot. It asks for a name, then a username ending in bot, and answers with the key.

Username taken? BotFather asks again — pick another one ending in bot.

You will see: a reply with a long line, numbers, a colon and a secret.

2 · Give viberoom the key

In Settings → Channels press Set up Telegram… and paste BotFather's message in the key field. viberoom takes the key out of it and starts the bot. The key is never shown to the vibemates and never enters the conversation, the record or a model's context.

You will see: Connected as @yourbot.

This step in the wizard

Lost or leaked the key? Send /revoke to BotFather and paste the new one in the same place; the old one stops working.

3 · Name the bot

One bot is one computer. Name it so the chats on your phone tell your computers apart: viberoom home, viberoom work. Anyone who opens the bot sees this name.

You will see: On the phone the bot is called “…”.

This step in the wizard

4 · Pair your phone

The wizard shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone's camera, then press Start in Telegram.

What pairs is a Telegram account: every device signed in to it can write to your rooms.

Anyone who opens the code pairs, so do not show it on a shared screen.

Close the wizard and the code is withdrawn, unless you copied the link.

You will see: Paired: your name on this computer, and Paired. on the phone.

This step in the wizard

5 · Open a room and talk

Send /rooms to the bot and press a room. Write a line; the vibemates answer there as they do here. While one is writing you see its reply grow, with a Stop button under it. /close detaches the chat; the room stays.

You will see: Opened <room>, then the last messages of that room.

6 · Files

A photo or a document you send from the phone lands in the room. A file named in a room gets a Send button on the phone, when it is in the room's own files, in its working folder, or in a folder you listed in Settings → Channels. No file leaves this computer without your press.

You will see: a Send <name> button under the message that names the file.

When something is different

  • Another computer is listening to this bot. One bot is one computer: make a second bot in BotFather for the other one. (Two viberooms on the same computer count as two, too.)
  • A vibemate is offline (after a restart): the bot says so and offers Reconnect.
  • The pairing code has run out (ten minutes): ask for a new one in the wizard.
  • A card you did not ask for: a card for the key or for pairing that you did not start, close it. The room's record says who asked.
  • Removing or moving viberoom: switch off Start viberoom when you sign in to this computer first, so the sign-in entry does not stay behind.

What this does not do

  • It does not give the vibemates any new access. What they may read or write is their own setting.
  • It does not hide your key from a vibemate that can read files on this computer; it keeps the key out of the conversation, the record and the model's context. If you ever suspect it, /revoke in BotFather ends it in seconds.
